System Overview: High-level brief of the cross-border mineral haulage operations across Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, and Zambia.
Departmental Modules & Dedicated Views: Detailed breakdown of each exclusive screen (Orders Board, Telematics Map, Financial Center, P&L & Border Dwell, Fuel History, Route Expenses, Petty Cash Float, Maintenance & Tyres, Reports, Files/POPs, and Driver In-Cab Console).
